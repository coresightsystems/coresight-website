CORESIGHT SYSTEMS

CoreSight Systems is a software company that builds a business operations platform for service and trade businesses — including roofing, HVAC, plumbing, and general contracting companies with up to 200 employees. The platform brings together customer relationship management (CRM), scheduling and dispatch, estimating and quoting, invoicing and payments, field operations, and reporting in a single system, helping these businesses manage leads, jobs, and customers from first call to final invoice. As part of the service, the platform sends SMS notifications — such as appointment confirmations and reminders, quote and proposal updates, and job status messages — to a business's customers and staff.
